I am an economist working at the border between theory and experiments. Much of my research asks how institutions shape collective decisions: how voting rules aggregate the information dispersed among voters, when they fail, and how people actually behave when those rules are taken to the laboratory.

I received my PhD from Universitat Pompeu Fabra in 2008. Before joining Queen Mary in 2013, I was a postdoctoral scholar at Caltech and then a research fellow at the Max Planck Institute for Research on Collective Goods in Bonn.
